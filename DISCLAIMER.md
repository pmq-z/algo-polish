- **Not affiliated.** Algo Polish is an independent project. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by YouTube or Google. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.
- **Automation.** The extension clicks buttons on the YouTube page on your behalf. Automated interaction may conflict with YouTube's Terms of Service. You use it at your own risk.
- **Queued removals resume.** Videos you discard stay queued even if you close the app, and are removed the next time you open it and choose that source. Use Undo on a queue row to stop one.
- **Rules and bulk actions can remove a lot at once.** Always read the preview. Topic chips and "barely watched" are heuristics based on titles and on-page data, and can be wrong. A backup file is saved first, but it only lists the videos. It cannot restore them.
- **Home feedback is a signal, not a switch.** "Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel" may be used by YouTube to tune your recommendations, and YouTube lets you clear them, but how much they change your feed is up to YouTube.
- **Removals are real.** Videos removed from your liked list or watch history are removed from your account. The extension cannot restore them (the undo only works before a removal starts).
- **Unsubscribing is real.** The Subscription Audit changes your actual YouTube subscriptions. The extension cannot undo it (the undo only works before the click happens). The backup file lists the channels and their links so you can subscribe again by hand, but notification settings and other per-channel choices are not saved. Channels are ranked by what appears in the Liked and Watch history scans you ran, which may be incomplete, so a channel you do watch may be listed as never watched. Review each card, and use Protect for channels you want to keep.
- **No guarantee on recommendations.** Cleaning your history may change what YouTube recommends, but how recommendations work is up to YouTube and results are not guaranteed.
